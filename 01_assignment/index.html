My Favorite Hobby: Coffee Brewing

Why I love Coffee Brewing

Making coffee is one of my favorite hobbies because first of all, I love drinking coffee. I used to buy coffee outside daily but it was very expensive on a daily basis so I bought the equipment needed so I can start making my own coffee. This is what started me on my coffee brewing journey.

Since I started making my own coffee, I have been able to experiment and find the perfect cup for my taste. I enjoy learning how small adjustments can improve the taste, aroma, and quality of every cup.

My Coffee Equipment Essentials

  • Freshly roasted coffee beans
  • Coffee grinder
  • Espresso machine
  • Digital weighing scale
  • Portafilter and tamper
